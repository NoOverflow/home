## YouTube
### BenEater

https://www.youtube.com/@BenEater

Amazing vulgarization of low-level machine architectures and protocols.
Must watch for pretty much all compsci ppl.

### Breaking Taps

https://www.youtube.com/@BreakingTaps

Material science, machining. Made his own litography machine and his own camera sensor.

### Chornobyl Family

https://www.youtube.com/@ChernobylFamily

Two guys from Slovakia working as on-site researcher. Extensive study of the Chernobyl NPP and incident with a strong focus on the mainframe used at the time (SKALA)
but also old soviet equipment: printers, hdd, terminals... Also has a really interesting video about the mainframe used by the DUGA radar (K340A)
